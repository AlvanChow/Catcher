Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Catcher collects through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you fill out our form, we collect your name, phone number, email, firm website and your answers to our questions. We also record the page you came from and any campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source).

How we use it

We use it to contact you about a call with Catcher, to prepare for that call and to improve our marketing. We do not sell your information.

Calls and texts

If you agreed to it on our form, we may call or text the number you gave us about your call, including with automated messages. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

Who processes it

Our website is hosted by Vercel, and form submissions are delivered to our team by email through Resend. These providers process the information only to provide their services to us.

Call Recording Notice

Calls that Catcher answers for law firms may be recorded and transcribed so the firm receives an accurate intake. Callers are told at the start of the call when it is recorded, as the law where they are requires.

Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone, including people who use a keyboard or a screen reader. If something doesn't work for you, email us and we'll fix it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you gave us, or to stop contacting you, by emailing hello@catchersolutions.com.
