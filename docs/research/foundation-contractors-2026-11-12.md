# Foundation contractors: pain points (research, 12 Nov 2026)

Who: foundation repair and leveling, piering, pier and beam, waterproofing and crawl spaces, and new foundation and slab crews.

| Pain point | What we found | How the lander answers it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jobs get shopped | Homeowners call several companies for quotes on a big repair; the first to respond usually gets the inspection | Headline and hero cards: every call answered in seconds |
| Real estate deadlines | Calls from pending sales or inspection reports with a closing date keep dialing until someone answers | Screening questions ask about home sale deadlines |
| Crews in the field | Most missed calls happen because the owner or crew is physically on a job | "Calls picked up while your crews are on site" |
| Shared paid leads | Angi/HomeAdvisor leads go to several contractors at once, so speed decides who wins | Implied: answer first, every time |
| Free inspection is the conversion | Every repair lead flows through a free inspection; inspection-to-sale is the real KPI | CTA and headline sell booked inspections, not calls |
| Demand swings | Leads spike after rain and soil movement, dry up in dry seasons | FAQ: busy weeks after heavy rain |
| No-shows and wasted drives | True cost per booked appointment is higher than owners think once no-shows and drive time are counted | Service-area check before booking |

Sources: see the chat reply of 12 Nov 2026 (Risacare, Signpost, Hook Agency, Leadtruffle, Customerflows/HBR, Clicksgeek, Eyefly).
Claims on the page stay qualitative; no third-party statistics are shown as fact.
